About Us

RCCG, Rain of Mercy Parish is an offshoot of RCCG, Jesus House for All Nations, under the leadership of Pastor and Pastor (Mrs) Jide Oyetuga and the guidance of our Zonal Pastor, Lekan Shaba. The parish was established on October 2, 2026.

The foundation of our church is laid on Jesus Christ our Chief Corner Stone and the Word of Life. We believe in the doctrines of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ and we emphasize the importance of holiness and faith as the key ingredients to having a successful Christian life.

The mercy of God, which is inexplicable and central to our existence on planet earth, gave birth to the name of the parish, because it is of God's mercy that we are not consumed.

Welcome to Rain of Mercy Parish, where the immeasurable mercy of God will be unleashed in your life.

Vision and Mission of RCCG

  • To make heaven.
  • To take as many people with us as possible.
  • To have a member of The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) in every family of all nations.
  • To accomplish no. 1 above, holiness will be our lifestyle.
  • To accomplish no. 2 and 3 above, we will plant churches within five minutes walking distance in every city and town of developing countries and within 10 minutes driving distance in every city and town of developed countries.
  • We will pursue these objectives until every Nation in the world is reached for the Lord Jesus Christ.
